Dear Editors,

I am pleased to submit the manuscript \emph{Exceeding Weak and Strong Unique Games Barriers for MAX-E2LIN(2) in Bounded-Degree Graphs} for consideration in \emph{Optimization Letters}.

The manuscript studies the approximability of MAX-E2LIN(2) on $d$-regular graphs and on graphs of maximum degree at most $d$, for every $d \ge 1$. Reducing an instance to a MAX-2SAT formula, passing to its conflict graph and applying a three-occurrence reduction, the lifted formulas admit a polynomial-time approximation ratio of at least $1 - \frac{1-\alpha_\varepsilon}{6d+12} > \frac{83}{84}$ on $d$-regular graphs and above $0.9932$ on arbitrary simple graphs. The manuscript shows that this exceeds both the weak Unique Games barrier $\alpha_{\mathrm{GW}} \approx 0.87856$ and the strong bounded-degree barrier $\alpha_{\mathrm{GW}} + \widetilde{O}(1/d)$, without contradicting the Unique Games Conjecture.

I believe the manuscript is suitable for \emph{Optimization Letters} because it contributes a focused result on approximation algorithms for classical combinatorial optimization problems (MAX-CUT, MAX-E2LIN(2) and MAX-2SAT) through semidefinite programming rounding and exact optimum identities, with a clear statement of how the guarantees relate to known hardness thresholds. The manuscript fits the journal either as a regular manuscript or as a short communication.

The manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration by another journal. It re-uses no previously published figures, tables or text, and it contains no unpublished material. It has a single author, who approves its submission. The Declarations section states that there are no competing interests and that no specific funding was received, and it contains the disclosure of the use of AI tools and the data and code availability statement.

Thank you for considering the manuscript. I look forward to your response.
